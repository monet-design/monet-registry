## Usage

These images should be placed in this directory (`/public/registry/smart365dental-team-2/`) and will be automatically loaded by the component.

## Alternative Options

If AI-generated images are not suitable, you can:
1. Use stock photos from sources like Unsplash, Pexels, or specialized medical stock photo sites
2. Use actual team photos from a dental clinic
3. Commission a photographer for professional team photos

Make sure any replacement images:
- Have transparent backgrounds (or can be easily removed)
- Show professional medical attire (white lab coats)
- Have appropriate composition (group of people standing together)
- Are properly licensed for your use case
